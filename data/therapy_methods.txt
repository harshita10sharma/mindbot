Therapy helps you explore your thoughts, emotions, and behaviors in a safe space. There are several types of therapy that support mental health:

1. **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT):** Focuses on changing negative thought patterns.
2. **Dialectical Behavioral Therapy (DBT):** Helps with emotional regulation and mindfulness.
3. **Psychodynamic Therapy:** Explores unconscious thoughts from past experiences.
4. **Humanistic Therapy:** Encourages personal growth and self-understanding.
5. **Group Therapy:** Offers shared healing experiences with others facing similar issues.
6. **Art or Music Therapy:** Uses creativity as a form of emotional expression.

Therapy is not a sign of weakness — it’s a powerful tool for self-understanding and emotional healing.
