Depression can make even small tasks feel impossible, but support and small steps can make a big difference.

1. Try to maintain a regular routine — even small wins count.
2. Spend time in sunlight or fresh air every day if possible.
3. Connect with friends, family, or support groups.
4. Avoid isolation — talking about how you feel helps.
5. Practice self-compassion — healing takes time.
6. Seek professional help when needed. Therapists and psychiatrists are trained to help you manage these feelings.

You are not alone — recovery is possible with patience and the right support.
