# Loneliness and connection

Loneliness is the painful gap between the connection we have and the connection we want. You can feel lonely in a crowd or content alone. Long-term loneliness affects sleep, mood and even physical health, so it deserves attention.

Small steps that help:
1. Reach out to one person this week, even with a short message.
2. Join a regular activity built around a shared interest, like a class, club, sport or volunteering. Repeated contact builds friendships more than one-off events.
3. Notice thinking traps like "nobody wants to talk to me" (mind reading). Test them with small experiments.
4. Be curious about others: ask questions and listen. Connection grows from small, repeated moments.
5. Limit passive social media scrolling, which can increase comparison and loneliness.

If loneliness comes with persistent low mood or hopelessness, talking to a counsellor can help.
