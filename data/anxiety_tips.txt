Anxiety can feel overwhelming, but small, consistent actions can help you feel more in control.

1. Practice mindfulness or meditation daily, even for a few minutes.
2. Challenge negative thoughts by writing them down and reframing them.
3. Limit caffeine and screen time, especially before sleep.
4. Stay connected with supportive people.
5. Try grounding techniques like focusing on your breathing or naming five things you can see.
6. Avoid perfectionism — progress matters more than perfection.

If anxiety continues to interfere with your daily life, seeking professional help is a sign of strength.
